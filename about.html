JOURNEY

Our Story & Evolution

The journey of NexaTech - from humble beginnings to industry leadership

2019 - Foundation

Started as a small automation consulting firm in Lagos, Nigeria. Began with manual process automation for local businesses, focusing on efficiency gains through simple scripts and workflows.

2020 - AI Focus

Expanded into AI solutions, working with machine learning models and predictive analytics. Developed first internal TikTok automation tool for content scheduling.

2021 - n8n Integration

Specialized in n8n workflow automation platform. Created complex multi-step automation pipelines for social media management and business processes.

2022 - TikTok Autoposter

Developed and launched NexaTech Autoposter - a sophisticated TikTok automation system with real-time analytics and smart content scheduling.

2023 - Scale Up

Reached 100+ active client campaigns. Expanded team to 10+ automation experts. Introduced enterprise-grade AI solutions for large corporations.

2024 - Global Reach

Expanded services to international markets. Launched API integration platform for third-party developers. Introduced AI-powered analytics dashboard.

2025 - Innovation Hub

Currently operating as a premier AI and automation innovation hub, serving businesses worldwide with cutting-edge solutions including advanced TikTok automation and custom AI implementations.
